Frequently asked questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are acoustic ceilings?

Acoustic ceilings are ceiling solutions designed to address acoustic considerations while also contributing to the visual design of an interior space.

Are acoustic ceilings suitable for commercial offices?

Yes. Acoustic ceiling solutions can be considered for corporate offices, commercial workplaces, meeting rooms, collaborative areas and other professional environments.

What acoustic ceiling designs does COFUR offer?

The COFUR acoustic ceiling collection includes Wave, Petal, Blade, Planks, Grid, Waffle and Hive designs.

Can architects and interior designers contact COFUR?

Yes. Architects, interior designers, procurement teams, contractors and workplace professionals can contact COFUR regarding commercial projects.

Can acoustic ceilings be combined with acoustic lighting?

Yes. Acoustic ceiling solutions can be considered alongside acoustic lighting and other workplace acoustic solutions depending on the project.

How can I request an acoustic ceiling quotation?

Submit your company and project details through the enquiry form. The COFUR team can then discuss your requirements and quotation.
