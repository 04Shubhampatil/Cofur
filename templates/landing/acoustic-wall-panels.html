Acoustic Wall Panels FAQs

Information for commercial project enquiries.

Which acoustic wall panel products does COFUR list?

The collection lists Grooved Wall Panels, Printed Wall Panels and Fluted Wall Panels. Visit each product page for the available details.

Which spaces are these wall panels intended for?

COFUR lists corporate, institutional and hospitality spaces as application areas for these wall panel products. Discuss your specific site and use case with the team.

Can I request a quote for a commercial project?

Yes. Use the enquiry form to share your contact information, project city, product interest and project stage.

Can architects and interior designers enquire?

Yes. Architects, interior designers, contractors and project teams can share their project brief through the enquiry form.

Do the panels have specified acoustic performance ratings?

Check the relevant product documentation or confirm with COFUR before relying on any particular acoustic rating or technical performance claim.
